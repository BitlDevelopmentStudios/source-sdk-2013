Alpha v3:

- Gordon Freeman's death camera has been set to the one used in Half-Life 2 and Episodes by default. This can be disabled with the cl_freeman_hl2deathcamera ConVar.
- Added a new map: hunt_lighthouse! A map that takes place on a lone Rebel base on the coast, watched over by a massive lighthouse. This map takes place in the same area as the coast lighthouse Gunship battle in Half-Life 2.
- Fixed a bug where dropped items respawned after usage.
- Improved Achievement Panel visuals.
- Added an infinite ammo admin command.
- Fixed a visual bug where you can hide the SMG1 viewmodel by right-clicking.
- Fixed a visual bug where Sniper Rifle bullets appeared to be shooting through walls, even though they weren't.
- Improved Sniper Rifle tracer visibility.
- Fixed awkward RPG laser dot placement.
- Fixed various texture compatibility errors with LUX.
- Overwatch Female Assassin overhauls:
	- Dual Pistols:
		- Each pistol now has their own accuracy penalty that applies the next time the pistol is shot.
		- The view punch now punches based on which pistol is fired.
		- Each pistol has a greater maximum accuracy penalty.
		- Bullets from each pistol now fire from the individual pistols' muzzles.
	- Sniper Rifle:
		- Increased Sniper Rifle range from 4096 hammer units to 16384 hammer units.
		- The Sniper Rifle's bullet speed will now increase based on range. This improves the timing and responsiveness between when the bullet is shot and when the bullet hits the target.
		- Sniper Rifles can now be recharged by Combine energy outlets which are plentiful wherever you petrol.
		- When unzoomed, the Sniper Rifle's bullets will now fire out from the muzzle.
		- The laser dot will now switch colors if the Sniper Rifle is not ready to fire yet.
